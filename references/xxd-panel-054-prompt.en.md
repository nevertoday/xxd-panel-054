## Aesthetic reconstruction

Do not describe the whole photograph. Compress the complex scene into three to six large shapes, establish one main visual that carries the narrative, then select exactly six genuinely memorable local details from the source and turn them into six stickers with clear hierarchy and unequal scale. Unite every element through matte gouache, cut-paper, Risograph, or screen-print material language to create a quiet, modern, relaxed independent-publishing memory page.

Follow this causal chain exactly: lock identity, silhouette, posture, and narrative relation → preserve three recognition cues → compress complexity into three to six large shapes → build one instantly recognisable main visual → choose exactly six source-specific memory fragments → vary sticker scale, angle, overlap, and hierarchy → unify through matte gouache and print texture → retain airy blue-led negative space → place minimal copy in the remaining rhythm.

## Hard visual requirements

- Preserve at least three source-specific cues from silhouette, proportion, posture, action, local structure, colour, material, or relation. Simplify the scene to three to six matte shapes while keeping instant recognition.
- Build one visibly dominant main visual. Then select exactly six source-supported memory fragments from features, gestures, structures, plants, objects, transport, food, patterns, materials, light, or incidental details. Give each sticker a thick pale cut border.
- Make the six stickers unequal in scale, rank, and visual weight. Arrange them asymmetrically in loose rows, edge clusters, or around the main visual, with slight rotation, overlap, or edge escape. Never form a product catalogue, six-cell grid, or equal icon set.
- Use one opaque gouache, cut-paper, Risograph, or screen-print material language across main visual and stickers: powdery edges, paper grain, hand-cut irregularity, and slight registration drift. Reject watery washes, dense linework, realistic texture, slick vectors, and 3D plastic.
- Lead with pale powder blue, mist blue, sky blue, and airy cool blue. Balance with ivory, cream, light beige, muted grey-green, and architectural neutrals. Allow only tiny dusty-rose or muted-blush accents where supported by the source.
- Control one main focus through scale contrast, positive-negative shape, overlap, and ample quiet ground. The six stickers must never compete with the main visual.
